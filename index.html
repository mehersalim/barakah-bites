Our Story

More Than a
Food Truck

Barakah Bites is a Houston-based halal food truck built on one belief: food is a blessing. The name says it all — barakah means divine blessing in Arabic, and that's exactly what we pour into every plate.

Located next to the Hamd Institute, we serve up bold, flavor-packed bites — from our legendary Dubai Burger to our crowd-favorite Chicken Loaded Fries — all 100% halal, always made fresh, served with warmth.

  • Fully halal-certified ingredients every single day
  • Outdoor seating — perfect Houston evenings
  • Fresh, made-to-order — no shortcuts, no compromises
  • Family-friendly atmosphere with community vibes
  • Serving the Westside of Houston since day one
